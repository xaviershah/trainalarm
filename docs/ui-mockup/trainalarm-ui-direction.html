TrainAlarm

Revision 2, with example data. Dot-matrix time readouts, a dial and ruler for picking the alarm, a map under the tracking sheet, and a glow on the alarm screen. Dark and light are both switchable in Settings.

Type: Anton for headlines and buttons, Doto (dot-matrix) for every time readout. Both are SIL OFL and would be bundled in both apps. Body text uses each platform's system font. Colour: the five reference colours, plus tints of them for surfaces and secondary text. All times and stations on this page are made up.
